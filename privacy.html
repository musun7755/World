Privacy Policy

HumanPuddi / HexingBot is a personal social media management tool used to manage the Threads account @humanpuddi.

This app may access Threads account information, posts, replies, mentions, and related interaction data only for the purpose of generating post drafts, detecting replies, creating reply drafts, and assisting account management.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

Stored data may include Threads post IDs, reply IDs, comment text, generated reply drafts, and processing status logs.

Data is stored locally by the account owner and used only for managing the @humanpuddi Threads account.
